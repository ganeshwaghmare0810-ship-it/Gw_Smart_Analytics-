Who We Are

About GW Analytics

GW Analytics is a data analytics platform created to provide a simple and user-friendly way to understand datasets.

Instead of looking at large amounts of raw information, users can explore important values through dashboards, charts, performance indicators and analytical summaries.

The platform is suitable for learning, academic projects, business analysis and data-driven decision making.
