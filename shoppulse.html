1. One Row Per Record

Keep one record per row (one sale, one student, one employee) with clean, unmerged column headers.

📅

2. Standard Date Formats

Use consistent ISO (YYYY-MM-DD) or DD-MM-YYYY formats, and keep currency numbers free of raw text characters.

🧹

3. Eliminate Blank Gaps

Avoid empty lines or random merged title rows in your data sheets to prevent parsing errors.

🔄

4. Regular Data Updates

A dashboard is only as good as the underlying data. Update daily or weekly for dependable business signals.

🎯

5. Focus on 4 to 6 KPIs

Too many numbers overwhelm the reader. Keep priority numbers on top and trends below.
